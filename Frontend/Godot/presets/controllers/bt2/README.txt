Built-in controller presets for Budokai Tenkaichi 2 (Dolphin GameCube pad profiles, .ini).
BT2 has its own button layout, so Tenkaichi 3's presets are not shared.
  PlayStation 4-5 (SDL)                                    DualShock 4 / DualSense (SDL)
  PlayStation 4-5 (DirectInput) - Recovery Fix             DualShock 4 / DualSense (DirectInput)
  PlayStation 5 (Windows Gaming Input) - Recovery Fix      DualSense (Windows.Gaming.Input)
  Xbox - Recovery Fix                                      Xbox 360 / One / Series (XInput)
"Auto" picks by the connected controller's name (the [Sparking] Match words): PlayStation pads get
the SDL one, Xbox pads the Xbox one. The DirectInput and Windows Gaming Input ones are picked by hand.
