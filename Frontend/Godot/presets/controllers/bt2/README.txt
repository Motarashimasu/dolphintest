Built-in controller presets for Budokai Tenkaichi 2 (Dolphin GameCube pad profiles, .ini).
BT2 has its own button layout, so Tenkaichi 3's presets are not shared.
  PlayStation 4-5 - Sparking Standard   DualShock 4 / DualSense (DirectInput)
  Xbox - Sparking Standard              Xbox 360 / One / Series (XInput)
"Auto" picks one by the connected controller's name (the [Sparking] Match words).
